## 🚀 About This Project

**Churn Analysis & Customer Intelligence** is an end-to-end Data Analytics project focused on understanding customer churn, identifying high-risk customers, analysing the reasons behind customer cancellations, and quantifying the potential revenue impact of churn.

The project simulates a real-world OTT subscription business where customer, subscription, and support data are stored across multiple relational tables.

The objective is not just to calculate churn, but to answer three important business questions:

> **Who is leaving?**  
> **Why are they leaving?**  
> **When are they most likely to leave?**

The analysis combines **SQL, Python, data cleaning, feature engineering, exploratory analysis, visualization, and business intelligence** to convert raw customer data into actionable retention strategies.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges for subscription-based businesses such as:

- Netflix
- Amazon Prime
- Disney+
- SaaS platforms
- Telecom companies
- Fintech platforms
- E-commerce businesses

Acquiring a new customer is often more expensive than retaining an existing one.

Therefore, the business needs to identify:

- High-risk customers
- Customer segments with high churn
- Plans/contracts associated with churn
- Geographic churn patterns
- Support issues linked to churn
- Revenue at risk
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) at risk
- The point at which customers enter the "danger zone"

## 🗂️ Dataset Structure

The project uses three relational tables:

### 1. Customer Table

Contains customer demographic and profile information.

- Customer ID
- Name
- Country
- State
- Gender
- Date of Birth
- Interests
- Pincode

### 2. Subscription Table

Contains customer subscription and revenue information.

- Customer ID
- Subscription Start Date
- Subscription Type
- Renewal Date
- Plan Type
- Contract Type
- Cancellation Date
- Cancellation Reason
- Monthly Charges
- CLTV
- Churn Score

### 3. Support Table

Contains customer support interaction information.

- Customer ID
- Complaint Date
- Escalations
- CSAT Score
- Comments
